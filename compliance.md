# Legal, Privacy and Compliance Register
Status: preliminary checklist only; not legal advice or a verified compliance assessment.

Do not treat prior jurisdictional summaries, retention periods, penalty amounts, EU AI Act interpretations, breach timelines or placeholder contact details in earlier drafts as authoritative. Applicability depends on actual entity, role, data types, processing purpose, location, contracts and current law; each requires verification against official current sources and qualified counsel where needed.

Before processing any customer or personal data:
1. Identify controller/processor roles and lawful purpose.
2. Obtain and record appropriate permission/consent where required.
3. Minimize collection and restrict access.
4. Define retention, deletion, backup and breach-response procedures.
5. Document subprocessors, hosting region and cross-border transfers.
6. Provide transparent AI-use disclosure where required.
7. Keep financial/tax/legal determinations under qualified human review.

Never use placeholder email addresses or claim certification/compliance without evidence. No customer data processing is authorized by this document alone.
